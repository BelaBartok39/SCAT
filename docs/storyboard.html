02

Home — the cylinder11 facets squared, wrapped on a cyclic axis

Live prototype
drag to rotate · arrow keys to move · enter to open
— hover or focus a cell — Fixed readout · never moves
  1. 1
    No text in cells. Click opens a modal. This removes the glyph-warping objection to a curved surface entirely.
  2. 2
    Crosshair + fixed readout. Curvature actively degrades header-tracing — column lines converge at the silhouette — so the readout sidesteps the task instead of fighting it.
  3. 3
    Hue = facet family (six groups, not eleven). Orientation while rotating, at a count perception can hold.
  4. 4
    Lightness = evidence density. The one thing position cannot tell you, and it makes the surface a research-gap map.
  5. 5
    The bright tile is Band × Objective — the shipped 45-cell matrix, already authored and cited. The published work sits inside the new work.
  6. 6
    The diagonal is excluded, hatched. A facet crossed with itself is a glossary entry, not a relation.
  7. 7
    Unroll to a flat grid — same data, same cells, one control. Cylinder to orient, grid to work. This transition is the paper's headline figure.
  8. 8
    Camera tilt is a readability constraint, not a taste call. Row labels sit at the silhouette; the front face — where the eye lands — sits lower by the tilt. The first build of this sheet used a steeper tilt and read one row wrong. Keep the offset under one row height.

Open — decide before step 04

Three unresolved items, ordered by how much they cost to get wrong.

Homeless

Where does the SPACECOM expansion live?

Link direction, GNSS civil/military rows, ISL and optical were the paper's contribution claim a week ago. Under the four-view architecture they have no view. Facet values in Home, a late addition to TRANSEC, or dropped.

Structural

Is Objective really the 11th facet?

It is absent from the sketch. If yes, Home subsumes the shipped matrix and the published work sits inside the new work. If no, TRANSEC stays a peer view beside it and the relationship is looser.

Load-bearing

Does the cyclic ordering hold?

Protocol → Band → Modulation → Antenna → Propagation → Noise → Attack → Objective → Mitigation → Limitation → Use-case → back to Protocol. The cylinder's entire justification rests on that loop being real rather than convenient. Pressure-test it before step 04 — if it fails, the honest answer is a flat matrix.
